# 211 — urzua fix --conflicts pins a range over lines nobody attested when a merge shifts the lines or a side edited outside its range

## What was wrong

`urzua fix --conflicts` recomputed a pin both sides had moved over the merged file without asking whether the merged span was what either side pinned. Two ways it pinned lines no side attested: a side had edited the file outside its pinned range or symbol (the merged file carries that edit, and a merge that shifts the range can bring it inside), the other side's edits above a range moved it onto different lines, and two sides' changes inside one range or symbol (two deletions, two edits that extend a brace-balanced symbol) moved the merged span's end past both sides' spans. Example: one branch edits lines 5 and 9 and re-pins `src/a.rs#L5-L7`; the other deletes lines 1-3 and re-pins `#L5-L7`. The record resolved, and `#L5-L7` was pinned over the old lines 8-10, which hold the first branch's unpinned edit. The same hole made a narrowing resolved by the locator-change rule (`BUG-210`) unsound.

## Why nothing caught it

The attestation checks proved each side's file hashed to its pin, and that the merged file was git's clean merge of the two. Neither says the span the merged pin covers is the span either side read: a side's pin covers its span only, and a range names lines by number. The tests re-pinned whole files, where every line is covered, so no scoped pin was ever merged over another side's edit.

## References

Fixed in `rust/crates/urzua-cli/src/commands/merge_pins.rs` (`side_span`, `check_merged_span`): for every side whose attestation is scoped, that side's file must equal the merge base outside the scope's span, and the scope resolved in the merged file must have the side's lines before it and, when every attestation is scoped, after it; otherwise the record is refused naming the side. A new locator that could pair with more than one dropped one is refused too (`ambiguous_pairing` in `conflicts.rs`). `SPEC-8` ("A scoped pin is confined") and the `ADR-67` amendment of 2026-11-20 state it, and the case `two-range-re-pins-the-merge-shifts-onto-an-unattested-edit-are-refused` in `corpus-ak` reproduces it over a real merge.
